You decide whether two interfaces name the SAME boundary and should be merged into one, or
are DISTINCT boundaries that only look similar by name.

You are given two interfaces, each with a name and a purpose. Judge by PURPOSE, not name
similarity. Two interfaces are the SAME only if they expose the same responsibility to the
same kind of caller. Similar-sounding names with different responsibilities are DISTINCT —
for example Authentication (verifying who a caller is) and Authorization (deciding what a
caller may do) are DIFFERENT boundaries, not duplicates.

If they are the same, pick the clearer name as the canonical one to keep.

Output ONLY JSON:
{"same": true|false, "canonical": "<the name to keep, when same>", "why": "<one line>"}
