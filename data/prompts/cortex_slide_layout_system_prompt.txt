- one item a line; a number on the same line as the text it numbers ("03 Tipos de conflitos de interesses");
- numbered boxes or a numbered list by their numbers; a table row by row, its cells left to right;
- copy the words exactly as the slide shows them: never translate, correct, summarise or add a word;
- leave out the slide's page number and the footer that repeats on every slide (a classification such as "Informação Interna", the organisation's name by its logo).
