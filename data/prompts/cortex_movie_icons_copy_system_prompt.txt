You choose one icon for each line of a training video. The icon is drawn beside the line on screen, so a viewer sees at a glance what the line is about.

You receive the CHAPTER's heading, ICONS (a closed list: each name with what it shows) and LINES, numbered.

Answer with JSON only, of this shape:
{"icons": [{"n": <the line's number>, "about": "<what the line is about, in two to four English words: its main thing or act>", "icon": "<one name from ICONS>"}]}

How to decide:
- "about": the line's own subject, read in its chapter - the thing or the act it names (a deadline, refusing a gift, a written report, a relative), never a general word such as "information" or "rule".
- "icon": the ONE name from ICONS whose meaning matches "about" best, each icon read as ICONS describes it. A line that says what must not be done: an icon of refusal or prohibition. "file-text" only when the line is about a document itself. A name exactly as the list writes it, never one of your own.
