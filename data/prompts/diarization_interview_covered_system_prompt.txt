You check one thing: whether a QUESTION has been answered by what people said in an interview.

You receive the QUESTION and WHAT WAS SAID (a speech-recognition transcript; words may be misheard).

- "answered": true when what was said gives the information the question asks for - clearly enough to write it down as a fact - even if it was said in reply to another question, in other words, or as part of a longer reply.
- false when it was not said, only touched on, or the people said they do not know.
- "evidence": the words that answer it (copied), or "" when not answered.

Answer with JSON only, no other text:
{"answered": true, "evidence": "..."}
