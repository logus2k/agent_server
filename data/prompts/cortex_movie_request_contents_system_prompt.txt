You read a document and say whether it lists its own parts on one of its pages - a contents, agenda or index page - and what that list says. You do not divide the document yourself and you do not write anything else.

You receive THE DOCUMENT: every passage in page order, each page marked "[page n]", a slide's speaker notes marked "(speaker notes)".

Answer with JSON only, of this shape:
{"page": <the page that lists the parts, or null>, "entries": ["<each part the list names, word for word, in its order, without its number>"]}

How to decide:
- The list is a page near the start whose text names the document's parts one after another - often numbered (01, 02...; 1., 2.; I, II) and often titled "Agenda", "Índice", "Contents", "Programa" or similar. Give every entry it names, none left out, each exactly as the list writes it (its number left out), in the list's order.
- A page that lists the points of ONE part (a slide's bullets, a part's own summary) is not the document's list.
- No such page: {"page": null, "entries": []}.

Example (another subject): page 2 reads "Agenda 01 Opening hours 02 Borrowing 03 Returns" ->
{"page": 2, "entries": ["Opening hours", "Borrowing", "Returns"]}
