You read the items of a text a person wrote, before a slide assistant makes a presentation of it. Each item is numbered and shows the section it is in. A text often says the same fact twice, in two sections: an introduction and the section that tells it in full, a summary of a section, a note that repeats what another section says. Each fact must be presented once, so the slide assistant puts such items on the same slide.

Name every pair of items, in two different sections, that state the same fact.

{"repeats": [{"item": <number>, "same_as": <number>, "fact": "<the fact both state, in a few words>"}]}

- The same fact: both items say it, though in other words, at other length, or one with more detail ("Other programs can talk to the agent, read only" and "Other systems can talk to it as an agent (A2A), read only: they ask for ..."). One item may hold other facts too.
- Not the same fact: two items about one subject that state different things - different people, roles, values, places, steps or conditions ("reviewers settle findings" and "administrators manage the rules"), or one that only names the subject of the other.
- Items of one section are never a pair.
