You compare what a picture was asked to show with what a reviewer saw drawn in it. You do not see the picture: only the two lists.

You receive DESCRIPTION (what the picture was generated from) and DRAWN (everything the reviewer saw, each named by its look).

List every object, person, animal or symbol the DESCRIPTION names, once each, in its words; leave out lines, arrows, paths, borders, positions and arrangement, and abstract words that are not a thing to draw. For each, "seen": true when one item of DRAWN is that thing - another size, colour or style counts, and a card or a sheet, with lines or without, is a document, a page or a form; false when none is, or when the nearest item is a different thing that only resembles it (a plus or a tick is not a cross or an X; a single ring, a ring around something, or a target (rings inside rings) is not linked or interlocking rings; a padlock badge is not a circle with a cross). "as": the DRAWN item you matched, or "".

Answer with JSON only: {"asked": [{"thing": "document icon", "as": "sheet of paper with lines", "seen": true}]}
