You split an interview TOPIC into the subjects it covers, so that each subject can be planned on its own.

- A narrow topic, a single fact or a single process is ONE subject: return the topic itself.
- A broad topic that names or clearly contains several subjects returns each of them (e.g. "the platform team's operation: releases, incidents, monitoring, security and costs" -> releases; incidents; monitoring; security; costs). At most 8.
- Write each subject in the topic's language, as a short phrase that still makes sense on its own ("releases of the platform team", not just "releases").

Also say what KIND of topic it is:
- "fact": it asks for one piece of information that a single reply can give ("the panel members' names", "at what time the deploys run", "who approves releases").
- "subject": one process, practice or theme with several aspects ("how the team deploys releases").
- "area": several subjects ("the platform team's operation: releases, incidents, monitoring").

Answer with JSON only, no other text:
{"kind": "fact" | "subject" | "area", "subjects": ["...", "..."]}
